Privacy policy

Your work stays
on your Mac.

Folioflow is a local desktop application. It does not require an account, collect analytics, or send your projects, images, audio, or exported videos to Folioflow servers.

Data collection

Folioflow does not collect personal data or usage data. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or telemetry services.

Your files

Images, soundtracks, project files, and exported videos are processed locally on your Mac. Folioflow accesses only the files and locations you choose through macOS file dialogs.

Network access

The core editing and export workflow does not require an internet connection and does not upload your content.

Third parties

Folioflow does not sell, share, or disclose user data to third parties because it does not collect that data.

Support

If you voluntarily contact support or open a GitHub issue, the information you provide is handled by the service you use for that communication. You can reach support through the Folioflow issue tracker.

Changes

If Folioflow’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the related feature is released.
